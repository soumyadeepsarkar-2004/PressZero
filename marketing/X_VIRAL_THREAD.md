### Tweet 2 (The Problem with Cloud Tools)
When you use tools like TinyPNG or random resizing websites:

1. Your sensitive Aadhaar, PAN, and passport are sent to foreign cloud servers.
2. You're forced to guess with vague quality sliders. 80% = 74 KB (rejected). 60% = 35 KB (blurry mess).

Neither gives you exact KB.

---

### Tweet 3 (The Binary Search Bisection Engine)
Instead of guessing, PressZero runs a binary search on the image's quantization tables directly in your browser via Web Workers.

You type 50 KB. The engine dynamically brackets the file and converges on 49.8 KB in < 80ms.

99.6% accuracy. Zero guesswork.
